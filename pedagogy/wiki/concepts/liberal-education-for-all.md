# Liberal Education for All

## Summary

The social thesis of [[sources/towards-a-philosophy-of-education-book-2|_Towards a Philosophy of Education_, Book II]] — and the book's subtitle. [[entities/charlotte-mason|Charlotte Mason]]'s claim: a generous, literary, humane education — the kind historically reserved for the leisured classes — is both the right and the capacity of _every_ child, in home schoolrooms, fee-paying schools, and elementary Council schools alike. The slogan she borrows for it is [[entities/comenius|Comenius]]'s _omnia omnibus omnino_ — "all knowledge for all men" — and she claims [[entities/parents-union-school|PUS]]'s thirty years of parallel results across social classes have at last made Comenius's ancient hope demonstrable in practice.

## Core Claims

- The same programme — the same books, the same questions, the same method of [[concepts/narration|narration]] after a [[concepts/single-reading|single reading]] — produces comparable results in fee-paying schools and in Gloucestershire Council schools under HMI H. W. Household. The difference of class is not a difference of mental capacity.
- [[entities/nfs-grundtvig|Grundtvig]] conceded, pessimistically, "all is not for all" — books, he thought, were not for the people, who must be taught by lecture. Mason disagrees: her evidence is that working-class children do read, narrate, and retain the same literary and historical matter that their middle-class peers do.
- The antithesis of this thesis is the _utilitarian_ Continuation School on the Munich model (Dr. Kerschensteiner): technical training for the labourer, literary education for the bourgeois. Mason rejects this explicitly as a betrayal of the person.
- Liberal-education-for-all is the practical content of the principle that [[concepts/children-are-born-persons|children are born persons]]. If a child is a person, no class of persons can be denied the food a mind requires.

## Connections

- The motto is [[entities/comenius|Comenius]]'s
- The foreign exemplar is [[entities/nfs-grundtvig|Grundtvig]]'s Danish People's High Schools
- The empirical vindication is the [[entities/parents-union-school|Parents' Union School]] and especially the Gloucestershire Council-school results
- Contrast: the utilitarian Munich Continuation School model
- Presupposes [[concepts/children-are-born-persons|children are born persons]] and [[concepts/knowledge-as-food|knowledge as the food of mind]]
- Operationally, the claim means [[concepts/living-books|living books]] and [[concepts/narration|narration]] for every child, not reading-schemes and dilutions for the "less able"
