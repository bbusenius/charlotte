# House of Education (Ambleside)

**Type:** organization (teacher-training college)

## Summary

The residential training college founded by [[entities/charlotte-mason|Charlotte Mason]] in 1892 at Ambleside in the English Lake District, where governesses and teachers were trained in the [[entities/pneu|PNEU]] method. It supplied both the [[entities/parents-union-school|Parents' Union School]] and the wider network of homes and schools with staff who had themselves lived under the programme, taken the single-reading-and-narration method as students, and sat the unrevised terminal examinations.

## Key Contributions / Characteristics

- A "practising school" attached to the college let student-teachers observe and conduct lessons under the same programme they had studied.
- Languages were taught at the House by the same method the students would use in their own classrooms: a _single reading_ of nine or so pages of Daudet or of a French history lecture, followed immediately by oral narration in the target language — tangible proof of the method for adults.
- Hosted the annual PNEU Conference.

## Connections

- Founded by [[entities/charlotte-mason|Charlotte Mason]]
- Run under the auspices of the [[entities/pneu|PNEU]]
- Supplies teachers to the [[entities/parents-union-school|Parents' Union School]] network
- A living application of [[concepts/narration|narration]] and [[concepts/single-reading|single reading]] to adult learning
