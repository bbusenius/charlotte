# Vannevar Bush

**Type:** person — engineer, science administrator, visionary

## Summary

Vannevar Bush (1890–1974) was an American engineer who, in his 1945 essay "As We May Think," described the **Memex** — a personal, microfilm-based device for storing and navigating a private library via "associative trails." The Memex is a conceptual predecessor to hypertext, the web, and (more directly) the [[concepts/llm-wiki-pattern]].

## Key Contributions

- **Memex (1945):** private, actively curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents
- Anticipated the core value of the LLM Wiki: connections between documents are as valuable as the documents themselves
- Could not solve the maintenance problem — who keeps the trails current. The LLM solves this.
