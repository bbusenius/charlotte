# Parents' National Educational Union (PNEU)

**Type:** organization

## Summary

The parent-led society founded by [[entities/charlotte-mason|Charlotte Mason]] in 1887 to disseminate her pedagogy through publications, conferences, regional branches, and — from 1891 — the [[entities/parents-union-school|Parents' Union School]]. The PNEU ran the _Parents' Review_, drew up the termly curriculum programmes used across its affiliated schools, and by the early 1920s counted affiliated homes, private schools, and state Council schools across Britain, the Dominions, and beyond.

## Key Contributions / Characteristics

- Produced termly **programmes** — detailed reading lists and question sets used identically in home schoolrooms, private schools, and elementary Council schools.
- Published the _Parents' Review_, the movement's long-running journal.
- Held annual conferences, notably the Ambleside Conference that convened at the [[entities/house-of-education|House of Education]].
- Claimed results independent of social class: PUS elementary Council schools in Gloucestershire under HMI H. W. Household achieved the same examination quality as fee-paying PUS schools — Mason treats this as the empirical vindication of her thesis that a [[concepts/children-are-born-persons|person]]-oriented [[concepts/living-books|living-book]] curriculum is universal.

## Connections

- Founded by [[entities/charlotte-mason|Charlotte Mason]]
- Operates the [[entities/parents-union-school|Parents' Union School]]
- Operates the [[entities/house-of-education|House of Education]] training college at Ambleside
