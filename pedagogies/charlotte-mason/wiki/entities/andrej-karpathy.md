# Andrej Karpathy

**Type:** person — AI researcher, educator

## Summary

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher known for his work on Tesla Autopilot, his co-founding role at OpenAI, and his accessible educational content on deep learning. In the context of this wiki, he is the originator of the [[concepts/llm-wiki-pattern]] — using LLMs as active maintainers of personal knowledge bases rather than passive retrievers.

## Key Contributions

- Described the LLM Wiki pattern as an alternative to RAG-based document Q&A
- Framed the human/LLM division of labor: human curates, LLM does all bookkeeping
- Connected the idea to Vannevar Bush's Memex as intellectual predecessor
