Quiet help for a home built on living books.

About

Charlotte is a Charlotte Mason homeschool companion. She keeps the ordinary record-keeping out of the way — lesson time, the books read and read aloud — and lends a hand with field trips, lesson and curriculum planning, and the small printed materials a home like this quietly accumulates.

Everything is grounded in the family's own pedagogy, so the help that comes back belongs in the atmosphere of the home rather than interrupting it.
