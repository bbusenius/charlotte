Charlotte

Quiet help for a home built on living books.

About

Charlotte is a homeschool companion, named for Charlotte Mason. She keeps the ordinary record-keeping out of the way — lesson time, the books read and read aloud — and lends a hand with field trips, lesson and curriculum planning, and the small printed materials a home like this quietly accumulates.

Everything is grounded in the family's own pedagogy, so the help that comes back belongs in the atmosphere of the home rather than interrupting it.

Tools

  • Spelling Helper A gentle helper for a young writer who has paused over a word — available for Android. Open →
  • Homeschool Dashboards Charlotte can generate local visual reports from the same Homeschool-Dashboard-compatible lesson-time and reading-list spreadsheets she helps maintain. These dashboards show class hours, daily work, curricula and materials, reading progress, and book lists. The dashboard renderer is Homeschool-Dashboard.
  • Family Schedules Forward-looking household rhythms and plans, kept separate from the lesson record.
